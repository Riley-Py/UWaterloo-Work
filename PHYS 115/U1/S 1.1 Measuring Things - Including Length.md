- **Unit**: Measure of a quantity (mass, time, length, pressure, etc.)
	- Attached to a **standard** - a physical quantity with 1.0 unit value 
		- Example: ${1.0 m} \over s$

## **Base Quantities & Standards**

- Independent quantities: Time, mass, weight, etc.
- Dependent quantities: Speed, velocity, etc.
- Seven fundamental quantities
	- Only three for mechanics: 
		- Length
		- Time
		- Mass
	- There are standards for each of them and define other physical quantities
- Base quantities are
	- Accessible
	- Invariable

## **International System of Units (SI)**

- Three base SI units
	- Meters (length) - m
	- Seconds (time) - s
	- Kilograms (mass) - kg
- Many derived units, written in terms of base units
	- Example #1: Joules (work - energy) - $1 J = 1 {}
