## **Mass Units & Measurement Standards**
- *Standard kilogram* - cylinder of platinum and iridium
- *Atomic mass unit* - 1 atom of Carbon-12 assigned 12 u
- Follows the same conversion process as lengths & time
## **Mass Vs. Density**
- *Density* - mass per unit volume, with formula: $$ \rho = {m \over v}$$
