## **Average Velocity**
- Displacement over object over time interval
- Used with this equation: $$ V_{avg} = {\Delta x \over \Delta t}$$
	- Where $\Delta x$ is the difference in displacement and $\Delta t$ is the difference in time
	- Units are in $m \over s$
