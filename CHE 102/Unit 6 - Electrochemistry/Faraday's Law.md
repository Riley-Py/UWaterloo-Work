**Electric Current** - Electric current (I) is the amount of charge (Q) flowing through a circuit per unit time (t) and can be measured using an ammeter.

$$I = {Q \over t}$$
$${ampere} {(A)} = {{coulomb (C)}\over {second (s)}}$$
Q is directly proportional to the amount of moles of electrons, where:
$$e = 1.6022137 \cdot 10^-19 C$$
## Faraday's Constant

It's the charge carried by one mole of electrons:
$$ F = 96 485 {C \over {mol}}$$
Therefore,
$$Q = Fn$$
And,
$$n = {Q \over F} = {It \over F}$$
