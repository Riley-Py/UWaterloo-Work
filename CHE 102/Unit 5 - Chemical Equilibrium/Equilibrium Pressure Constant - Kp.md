For the reaction described by the balanced equation: 
$$aA +bB \rightleftharpoons cC + dD$$
The equilibrium constant with respect to pressure, $K_p$, is:
$$ K_p = {{{P_C}^c{P_D}^D}\over {{P_A}^a{P_B}^b}}$$
**Note**:  The relationship between $K_p$ and [[Equilibrium Concentration Constant,  Kc|K_c]] is:
$$K_c = K_p(RT)^{-\Delta n}$$
where:
$$\Delta n = c + d - (a + b)$$
and $R = 0.08314 L bar \cdot mol^-1 K^-1$
