**Definition of relative humidity**: It's the ratio of the partial pressure of water vapour in the mixture to the equilibrium vapour pressure of water at that temperature with the formula: 
$$\% RH = {P_{H_2O} \over {P_{H_2O}}^{vap}(T)} \times 100 \%$$

**Dew point temperature**: The temperature at which humid air reaches saturation and condenses to form the first drop of liquid water ($T_{dp}$)

For gases other than water vapour, *relative saturation* is used rather than *relative humidity*

**Definition of relative saturation**: The ratio of the partial pressure of component A (A as a gas) in the mixture to the equilibrium vapour pressure of component A at that temperature.  It's with this formula:
$$\% saturation = {P_A \over {P_A}^{vap}(T)} \times 100 \%$$
