When a substance undergoes a phase transition, it usually takes up or releases energy.  The energy released during condensation is the **enthalpy of condensation**.  The reverse reaction, vaporization, requires energy to be added as heat to maintain a constant temperature.  This energy is the **enthalpy of vaporization**.  This is when a given quantity (1 mol or 1 kg) is vapourized (condensed)

$$\Delta H_{vap}=H_{vapour} - H_{liquid} = -\Delta H_{cond}$$
$\Delta H_{vap}$ decreases with increasing temperature as intermolecular forces decreases with increasing temperature. Thus, less energy must be absorbed to overcome these intermolecular forces.
