## Terms/Concepts
- *Earth Science Literacy* - Earth influences you and you influence the Earth
- *Scientific Method* - Acquiring knowledge in natural science through observation and experimental testing of hypotheses
- *Geology* - Study of Earth's composition/structure, and the processes that contribute to these and Earth's history/time from the origin
- *System* - Connected components that are bounded and work together. Earth is considered a system.
- *Geologic Time* - Long period of time that represents Earth's geologic history.
