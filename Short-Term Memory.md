- People recall more at the beginning or the end of the list rather than in the middle of the list is called *serial position effect*
	- Improved recall at the beginning is called *primary effect*
	- Improved recall at the end is called *recency effect*
- *Rehearsal* - repetition of items
- Recency effect is from using sensory or short-term memory
- Primary and recency effects reflect two different kinds of memory, as they can be independently effected 
- Short-term memory is used for phone numbers; it only lasts for a minute or two if rehearsal is not prevented
