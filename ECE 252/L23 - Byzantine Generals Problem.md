### Layout of Problem
- *General* - gives orders
- *Lieutenants* - commanding group of Empire's troops
- *Troops* - do as they are told
- Lieutenants can communicate with each other through messages; get orders from general
- General or lieutenants can be disloyal or loyal
	- In computing terms: "loyal" is "functioning" and disloyal is "faulty"
- Disloyal general sends different messages to different lieutenants
