## Networks of Visual Attention
- 32 areas of the brain become active during visual processing of an attended stimulus
- Posner argued that there are three mental operations to perform a task
	1. *Disengage* attention from wherever it was previously
	2. Attention then must be *move* to the attended stimulus
	3. Neural processing of the new location must be *enhanced*
