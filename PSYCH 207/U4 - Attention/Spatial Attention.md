- *Visual search*: Finding something using your eyes
- *Spatial cue*: Directing one's attention to something visually (i.e. a light flash)

## Spatial Cueing
- Ponser's Research showed with different cues that 80% of the trials with the one-headed cue, the target appeared in the same location by the cue (*valid cue*); in 20% of the trials, the target appeared opposite that of the cue (*invalid trial*); in the case of the two-headed cue, targets are likely to appear on either side *neutral trial*
	- Developed the spotlight metaphor, which goes "attention can be likened to a spotlight that enhances detection of events within its beam"
## Visual Search
- Experiment involving finding a letter that is colored showed that detection of individual features is automatic (not requiring attention)
