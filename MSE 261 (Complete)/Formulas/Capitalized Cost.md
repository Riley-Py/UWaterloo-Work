- Annual cost is constant over time, *capitalized cost* is present value of uniform series over infinite time periods
### Cost without a growth factor
$$ P = {A \over i}$$
Where:
- $P$ is present value
- $A$ is payments made at regular intervals
- $i$ is interest rate

### Cost with a growth factor
$$P = {A \over {i - g}}$$
Where:
- $g$ is percentage of growth over time
- Rest of variables are the same
