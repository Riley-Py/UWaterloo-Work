## Key Terms & Concepts

- *Natural Hazard* - Event caused by Earth processes (not humans) that threatens human life/property
- *Natural Disaster* - Event that negatively impacts humans (death) or property (buildings/road damage) over a limited time/area
- *Natural Catastrophe* - Event that negatively impacts humans/property beyond the directly affected area (regional to global) that requires extended time to recover
- *Earthquake* - Sudden movement of Earth material (sediment/rock) on opposite sides of a fault that releases energy/produces vibration of Earth
- *Mass Movement* - Downslope movement of Earth materials caused by gravity
- *Earthquake Belt* - Area with high concentration of earthquakes
