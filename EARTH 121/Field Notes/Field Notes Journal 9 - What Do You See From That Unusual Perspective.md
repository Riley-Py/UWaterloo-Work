## Response

What I see from this unusual perspective of the Great Lakes is with regards to the atmosphere.  I see the sun reflecting off of the Great Lakes and clouds made of water covering them, reminding one of the greenhouse effect that the sun and water vapour contribute to, connecting to the atmospheric system of Earth.  As well, seeing the Great Lakes from this view makes one think of the freshwater that is essential to human life, and how living organisms have come to rely upon water bodies like these to survive, connecting to the biosphere of the Earth.  Finally, the way the Great Lakes are shaped and formed remind one of how water breaks down rocks and how it builds large reservoirs like the Great Lakes, connecting it to the geosphere of Earth.
