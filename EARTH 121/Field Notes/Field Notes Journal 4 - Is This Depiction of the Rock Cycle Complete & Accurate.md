## Impact of Incomplete Rock Cycle (example of Systems Thinking) (300 Words Max)

The impact of an incomplete rock cycle for learning about the systems of the Earth is substantial.  The original illustration was meant for children to learn about the rock cycle in a simplified manner.  The problem with simplifying the rock cycle is that it loses the fact that the rock cycle is interconnected and has many parts that connect to one another.  For example, in the original image, it shows the rock cycle starting from the magma phase and going to igneous rock, then going to sediment, then going to sedimentary rock, and then metamorphic rock in that order.  However, in reality, the rock cycle doesn't have specific steps, and can jump between phases through different processes.  This can lead children to think that the rock cycle is a rigid structure, but in reality is quite flexible depending on the processes done.
