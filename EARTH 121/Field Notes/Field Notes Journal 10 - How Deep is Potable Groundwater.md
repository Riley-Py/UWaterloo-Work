## Part 3: Paragraph

The potential groundwater source for the waterwell location with granite would be from the fact that the granite could be faulted or when the granite is sheared.  This is the reason why the depth to water is the 2nd largest; granite doesn't store water directly, but only when it's broken.  Additionally, the location of the water well is within the Canadian Shield, where the rocks aren't porous.  The potential groundwater source for the water well location with limestone would be within the limestone itself, as limestone is quite porous as a material.  Therefore, you don't need to drill too deep in this location to find groundwater.  The location is in an area where many porous rocks are found, so this also aids in finding groundwater easily. Finally, the potential groundwater source for the location with sand would be quite deep, as shale and sand allow water to drain quite far (very porous)
