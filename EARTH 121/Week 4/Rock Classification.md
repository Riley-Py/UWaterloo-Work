# Key Terms + Concepts
- *Texture* - Size, shape, distribution of particles that collectively constitute a rock
- *Foliation* - Planar arrangement of textural features exhibited by metamorphic rocks
- *Dyke* - Tabular-shaped intrusive igneous feature that cuts through the surrounding rock
- *Phaneritic texture* - Igneous rock texture in which crystals are roughly equal in size and large enough so the individual minerals can be identified without magnification
- *Aphanitic texture* - Igneous rock texture in which crystals are too small for individual minerals to be distinguished without magnification
- *Felsic composition* - Compositional group of igneous rocks indicating rock is composed almost entirely of light-coloured silicates
