## What is the Most Abundant Mineral Family or Class Found in Earth's Crust?
- Most abundant mineral family are the silicates
	- Consists of the most abundant elements in [[Elements|Earth's crust]] 
	- Silicon and oxygen bond to form [[Introduction To Rocks|silicate tetrahedron]] 
		- Bonds with other elements, like aluminum and iron
	- Diagram of this: 
		- ![[silicates.jpg]]
	- Subdivided into two other categories:
		- *Ferromagnesium silicates* - darker colored, higher specific gravity
			- Example: Biotite, which is black/brown/dark green and has a specific gravity of 3.0
		- *Non-ferromagnesium silicates* - ligher colored, lower specific gravity
