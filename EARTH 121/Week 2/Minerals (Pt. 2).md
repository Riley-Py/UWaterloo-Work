## How Do Geoscientists Define Minerals?
- Each mineral has five key characteristics to be defined as a mineral
	1. *Naturally occurring*
	2. *Inorganic*
	3. *Solid*
	4. *Ordered internal molecular structure*
	5. *Definite chemical composition*
- Geoscientist uses a few tools to identify minerals, such as:
	- A hand lens to see crystal geometries
	- A scribe with magnetic capabilities to test hardness/magnetism
	- A porcelain plate to test [[Introduction To Rocks|streak]]
