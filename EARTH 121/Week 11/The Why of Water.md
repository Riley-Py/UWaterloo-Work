## Key Concepts/Terms
- *Electromagnetic Radiation* - Form of energy that travels at speed of light that is categorized by its wavelength in EM spectrum (visible/infrared)
- *Stromatolite* - Communities of microorganisms that trap and precipitate particles in microbial mats in shallow water environments
- *Koppen Climate Classification* - Describes climate zones across Earth based upon vegetation, precipitation, temperature
- *Topography* - Relief of elevation of exposed continental land surface above a reference elevation or datum such as mean sea level in ocean
- *Bathymetry* - Depth of water below a reference elevation or datum such as mean sea level in ocean
- *Hypsographic Curve* - Plot of cumulative area on the Earth's surface that occurs at various elevations relative to average sea level
- *Firn* - Granular re-crystallized snow (transition between snow and glacial ice)
- *Alpine glacier* - Mass of glacial ice in elevated terrain or mountains that descends down valleys
