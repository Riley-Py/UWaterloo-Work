## Key Terms/Concepts

*Resource* - Earth's natural material identified, available to be extracted profitably, and useful to humans
*Banded Iron Formation (BIF)* - Chemical sedimentary rock, with alternating bands of iron oxide minerals and chert.  Restricted to Precambrian and is prolific in Proterozoic rock sequences
*Volcanogenic Massive Sulphide (VMS)* - Hydrothermal mineral deposits composed of sulphide minerals associated with volcanic rocks
