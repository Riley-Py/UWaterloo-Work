## Key Terms/Concepts

*Resource* - Earth's natural material identified, available to be extracted profitably, and useful to humans
*Banded Iron Formation (BIF)* - Chemical sedimentary rock, with alternating bands of iron oxide minerals and chert.  Restricted to Precambrian and is prolific in Proterozoic rock sequences
*Volcanogenic Massive Sulphide (VMS)* - Hydrothermal mineral deposits composed of sulphide minerals associated with volcanic rocks
*Skarn* - Metamorphic rock deposit associated with contact metamorphism caused when an igneous pluton intruding into carbonate sedimentary rock layers
*Fracking* - Fracture rock by injecting pressurized liquid in a well drilled into Earth that forces a pathway to extract oil/gas
*Smelting* - Extract metal from its ore by melting
*Slag* - By-product left over after metal has been separated through the processes of smelting from its ore
*Aggregate* - Non-renewable, non-metallic mineral resource consisting of any combination of sand, gravel, crushed rock
