- Two categories:
	- *Metallic*
	- *Non-metallic*
- Diagram of the differences between them: 
	- ![[metallic-non-metallics.jpg]]
### Metallic
- Contain metal/opaque solid
- Lustrous/shiny
- Conductor of heat/electricity
- Pressed into sheets (*malleable*) or drawn into wire (*ductile*) without breaking/cracking

### Non-Metallic
- Not used for fuel/processed for metal
- Divided into three categories
	- *Agricultural* - food production (i.e. apatite in fertilizers)
	- *Construction* - building (i.e. calcite in cement)
	- *Industrial* - chemical/manufacturing (fluorite to manufacture steel)

## Native Metal & Ore
- *Native metal* - look and behave like metal, disengaged from ore
- *Ore* - source of mineral/metal in bedrock
	- Metal is removed by smelting, and produces a non-metallic residue called slag
