
## Key Terms and Concepts
- *Rock Cycle* - Model that shows the origin of three basic rock types and how it relates to Earth materials and properties
- *Sedimentary Rock* - Formed from the weather products of preexisting rocks that have been transported, deposited, and transformed into stone
- *Metamorphic Rock* - Formed by alteration of pre-existing rock deep within Earth (but still in the solid state) by heat, pressure, and/or chemically active fluids
- *Igneous Rock* - Formed from the crystallization or solidification of magma or lava
- *Magma* - Body of molten material found at depth, including any dissolved gases/crystals
- *Sediment* - Material that settles out in particulate form from a fluid, from gravitational factors/preciptation
- *Lithification* - Cementation or compaction
