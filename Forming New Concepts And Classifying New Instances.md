## Concept Attainment Strategies
- Studies found multiple strategies for categorizing different figures into groups
	- *Simultaneous scanning* - People used each card to test and rule out multiple hypotheses.  Difficult to use, and a lot of demand on working memory
	- *Successive scanning* - participants tested out a hypothesis one at a time.  Less efficient, but more manageable 
	- *Conservative focusing* - Finding a card that illustrated the concept (*focus card*), then testing other cards that varied from it in one aspect.  Efficient, but cards need to be laid out in an orderly fashion
- Valid instances of the category share necessary and sufficient features
	- Called *nominal* - concepts that have precise definitions
