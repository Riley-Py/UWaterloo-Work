- Research is based around [[Self-Determination Theory]] 
- SDT says that need supports enhances intrinsic motivation, which results in higher achievement
	- Controlling achievement outcomes via extrinsic rewards lead to lower-quality motivation and performance
### Intrinsic/Extrinsic Motivation within SDT
- Play, exploration, and curiosity activities are intrinsic behaviours (i.e. done for fun)
- Studies found that intrinsic motivation was associated with higher performance in formal education, regardless of geographic region/culture
	- Intrinsic motivation declines over the years
