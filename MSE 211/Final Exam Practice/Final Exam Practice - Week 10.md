### Answer to Q1

Technical leaders are effective as not being the most knowledgeable people within the organization, as they aid in creating a culture within the organization that is iterative and is subject to feedback.  This allows individuals on the team to buy into the mission that the leader is trying to instil upon them.  An example of this would be within a software engineering team, where technical leaders aren't involved in C-Suite decisions, but are instead on the front lines, encouraging feedback about programming quality and taking that feedback to create better solutions.  As a result, individuals on the team are more likely to look up to the leader and respect them.  Thus, the claim that the most technical leaders are not necessarily the most knowledgeable people in the organization may be true, but it's severely lacking in regards to decision authority.  Technical leaders may not have control over what happens to their team, as they don't have the authority that the knowledgeable people within the organization - the C-Suite executives - have.
