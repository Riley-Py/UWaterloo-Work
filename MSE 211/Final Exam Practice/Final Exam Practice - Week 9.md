To do so, they start to emphasize to team members about being stable when it comes to execution, and demands reporting that shows that the uncertainty and pressure is getting dealt with.  Team members perceive this as not being able to voice valid concerns about the organization, thus they start to signal less and less to leadership about the state of their team.  As well, they start to become cautious and fearful of raising concerns that raise uncertainty.
