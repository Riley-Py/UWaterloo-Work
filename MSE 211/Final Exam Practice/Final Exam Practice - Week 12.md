
Evaluate the claim:

"Most organizational problems are better explained by system conditions than by individual characteristics"

- Identify where the claim is strongly supported (bounded rationality, distributed attention, information flow, culture, assumptions, authority versus expertise, psychological safety, organizational dependencies)
- Identify where the claim requires qualification (competence, expertise, individual judgement, ethics, leadership decisions, professional responsibility, deliberate misconduct)
- Use at least one example (engineering, healthcare, software, research, infrastructure, government, another technical environment)
- Evaluate using explicit criteria, such as:
	- Explanatory power
	- Predictive value
	- Realism
	- Usefulness 
	- Organizational applicability
