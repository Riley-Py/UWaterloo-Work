### Answer to Q2

Explaining the full mechanism, from system conditions to the outcome, starts with the working conditions that the organization is working under.  They are operating under uncertainty and pressure to remain competitive in the market.  In response, they make it their goal to make things certain and to alleviate the pressure whilst also being the top of the market.  They think that if they do this, then their outlook is bright and they will be able to dominate.  Thus, they interpret any bad news that comes their way as bad, and some individuals within the organization start to dominate with opinions that match the perceptions of the organization.  For the individuals within this team, they take this as not feeling like they can speak their mind and voice any concerns that the organization may be facing in fear of reprimand.  Gradually, this leads to less participation from team members about the true state of the organization, and the organization thus starts to not coordinate with teams, but the teams become detached.  This breaks down the communication, and the information that does travel to the organization is overly optimistic, as it aligns with the goals of the organization.  Team members aren't able to voice any concerns to management, and evaluation of progress on the team degrades to being one-sided.  The faulty evaluations leads members who are typically experts within their respective fields to make erratic decisions, such prioritizing quality over quantity.  Thus, they start taking short cuts within the pipeline, leading to poor outcomes to the organization.  Expertise in this scenario did not prevent failure, as the members who were experts within their field fell victim to conforming to the group collective, and thus weren't able to voice their concerns based on their expertise.  Participation narrowed as team members figured that if the reports weren't positive, then they would be ignored.  This created an unsafe work environment and made individuals feel as though that their opinion didn't matter.  Disagreement was difficult, as leadership suppressed any disagreement with reprimand.  Information became filtered as opportunistic reporting didn't reveal the full extent as to what was happening within the organization, and that teams started to not cross-communicate with one another.  Collective intelligence degraded, as members felt as though they couldn't voice any novel ideas that weren't completely aligned with the goals of the organization.  The outcome was system-consistent, as the organization was operating under the pressure of competition, thus they wanted to alleviate that pressure through any means necessary, including suppression of critiques/ideas not aligned with their goals.
