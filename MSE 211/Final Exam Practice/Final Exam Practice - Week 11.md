### Answer to Q1

Organizations usually struggle more with implementing the right solutions, as it may take time and effort to put those solutions to action.  There may be many different levels of the organization that may have to travel through, and the solution may not be consistent with the culture that the organization has developed.  An example of this would be a family ran engineering organization, where the structure is bureaucratic and the top person is the one making the final decision.
