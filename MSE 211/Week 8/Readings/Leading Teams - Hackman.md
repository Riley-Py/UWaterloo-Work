- With assertive leadership, creativity, agility, and learning can happen alongside consistency, control, and fulfilment of collective objectives
- An effective team does these things:
	1. Serve customers well
	2. Become more capable over time
	3. Provide settings where individual members can find their teamwork as a measure of personal learning/fulfillment

### Serving Customers
- Productive output of team exceeds standards of quantity, quality, and timeliness of *customers* (people who receive, review, use output)
- Client's standards/assessments that count; not of the team's standards/assessments
- Manager's job is to help team identify standards used by real customers, and do whatever can be done to help team meet standards
