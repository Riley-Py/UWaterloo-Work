### Introduction
- *Employee performance optimization* - analyzing and improving individual workers
- Studies show that groups innovate faster, see mistakes more quickly, and find better solutions to problems
	- Also achieve better results and report higher job satisfaction
	- Profitability also increases with collaboration
- Google initially thought that the best teams are made out of best people
	- This wasn't true, however, and that is how Project Aristotle took form: to study teams and figure out why some are great and some are bad

### Project Aristotle
- Impossible to find patterns when it came to composition of team
- *Group norms* - behavioural standards/unwritten rules that govern how groups function
	- Encourages deference to team/overpowers individual opinions
- Understanding/influencing group norms were key to improving Google's teams
- Studies found that teams that did well on one assignment did well at the others and the inverse was true
	- Due to how teammates treated one another
- Two behaviours that all good teams shared:
	1. Members spoke in same proportion
	2. Knew how others felt based on tone of voice, expressions, and other nonverbal cues
- These behaviours constitute *psychological safety*
