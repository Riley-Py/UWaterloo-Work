### Abstract
- Found that top and middle managers' shared emotions during smartphone innovation process caused cycles of behaviours that harmed both process/outcome
	- Top managers were afraid of external competitors/shareholders
	- Middle managers were afraid of internal groups (superiors, peers)
	- Top managers' fears led to exert pressure on middle managers and misinterpret communication by middle managers
	- Middle managers didn't share negative info with top managers, leading to top managers thinking that their tech capabilities were fine without investing into innovation
