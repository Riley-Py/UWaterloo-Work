- *Social Loafing* - Individual effort is decreased when performing in groups as opposed to performing individually
- In maximizing task, success depends on how much something is accomplished and how much effort is expended
	- In optimizing task, precision, accuracy, or correctness are more important
- *Unitary task* - task that can't be separated into subtasks
- *Additive task* - task depends on sum of individual efforts
	- Therefore, if three people were pulling on a rope, they would be able to exert three times as much force as one person
- Research found that people collaborating in groups didn't exhibit the additive effect predicted
	- Called the *Ringelmann effect*

### Experiment 1 Clapping Hands -Results/Discussion
- More people clapping, more noise produced
	- However, it didn't grow in proportion to number of people
	- Sound pressure per person decreased with increased group size
- *Social facilitation theory* - people are aroused by others and are likely to work harder when together
- Group product may have suffered due to inefficiency within group
	- Due to no social coordination

### Experiment 2 Coordination Loss or Reduced Effort - Results/Discussion
- People had more intensity shouting alone rather than shouting in a group
	- Due to not being influenced by the output of others, lifting pressure of social conformity
- Again, the more people shouting, the total sound output increased, but not at the expected rate
