- Challenge in persuasion work is getting things done through others
	- Persuasion skills exert greater influence over others' behaviour than formal power structures
- Persuasion appeals to set of human drives/needs in predictable way
- There are six principles of persuasion

### Principle of Liking
- People like those who like them
- To influence people, win friends
- Two factors to be liked
	- *Similarity* - literally draws people together; creates bonds with hire/head of other department/new boss
	- *Praise* - charms and disarms
		- Positive remarks about person's traits, attitude, performance generates liking in return
		- Also can make it so that a person who doesn't like a boss/manager can start to warm up to them
