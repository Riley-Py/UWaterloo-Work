### Introduction
- *Great man hypothesis* - history is shaped by forces of leadership
- *Trait theory* - leadership depends on personal qualities of leader; doesn't reside within a few people
- Findings found that leadership isn't passive and doesn't have a specific combo of traits
	- Trait's effect on leadership behaviour depend on situation
- There were a review of traits, and found that, except for self-confidence, there was no trait that emerged as a common trait
- Dominance and masculinity/femininity related to leadership emergence some studies
