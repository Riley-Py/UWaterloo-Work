- Transactional leader practice reinforcement of followers; transformational leader inspire, stimulates, and is considerate of followers
- Can be directive/participative
### Introduction
- Transformational leaders uplift morale, motivation, morals of followers; transactional leaders cater to followers' self-interests
- Transformational leadership fosters autonomy and challenging work for job satisfaction; transactional leadership isn't the only thing for job satisfaction

### Full Range of Transactional and Transformational Leadership
- *Transactional leadership* - exchange relationship between leader/follower to meet own self-interests; examples include
	- Rewards
	- Leader monitors follower's performance; take corrective action if follower fails to meet standards
	- Leader waits for problems to arise before taking corrective action or doesn't take action at all
- *Transformational leadership* - leader moving the follower beyond self-interests through idealized influence (charisma), inspiration, intellectual stimulation, or individualized consideration
	- Idealized influence/inspirational leadership are displayed when leader envisions great future, talk about how it can be reached, sets example to be followed, sets high standards of performance, and shows determination and confidence
	- Intellectual stimulation is displayed when leader helps followers become more innovative/creative
	- Individualized consideration is displayed when leaders pay attention to developmental needs of followers; support and coach the development of followers
- Members of transformational teams care/intellectually stimulate/inspire each other and identify with team goals; high performing

### Going Beyond Self-Actualization
- Related to [[Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs|maslow's hierarchy of needs]] 
- Transforming leader moves followers up Maslow's hierarchy
- Transformational leader needs to align followers' self-interests in their own development with the interests of the group

### Related Concepts And Measures
- Transformational leaders can be directive (authoritarian) or participative (democratic)
- *Leader-member exchange (LMX)* - refer to [[Leadership Review - Yukl|leadership review]] 
- *Individualized consideration* - concern for each follower as an individual and with follower's development
