### Introduction
- Transformational leaders uplift morale, motivation, morals of followers; transactional leaders cater to followers' self-interests
- Transformational leadership fosters autonomy and challenging work for job satisfaction; transactional leadership isn't the only thing for job satisfaction

### Full Range of Transactional and Transformational Leadership
- *Transactional leadership* - exchange relationship between leader/follower to meet own self-interests; examples include
	- Rewards
	- Leader monitors follower's performance; take corrective action if follower fails to meet standards
	- Leader waits for problems to arise before taking corrective action or doesn't take action at all
- *Transformational leadership* - leader moving the follower beyond self-interests through idealized influence (charisma), inspiration, intellectual stimulation, or individualized consideration
