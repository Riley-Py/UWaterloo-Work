### Wells Fargo Culture, Values, Management
- Known for avoiding rest of industry's mistakes
- Success was built on cultural/economic model, combining customer relations with engaged sales culture
	- "Doing the right thing and doing it in the right way" was their slogan
- More products that a customer has, more information the bank has on customer
	- Better decisions about credit, products, pricing
- Decisions were made collectively and never alone
- Employee engagement scores were high among other companies
### Cross-Selling Scandal
- Employees were rumoured to using aggressive tactics to meet cross-selling targets
- Observers stated how setting daily sales targets puts pressure on employees
	- Branch managers were assigned quotas for number/type of product sold
	- If not met, shortfall was added to next day's goals
	- Employees were provided financial incentive to cross-sell
- Executives denied that this was a problem, as they had checks and balances that would prevent this from happening
	- Handbooks stated that it was a sales integrity violation
	- Ethics program instructed employees on spotting
