### Overview of Theory
- *Two-factor content theory* - motivation can be divided into hygiene factors and motivation factors; two separate needs
	- Need to avoid unpleasantness/discomfort
	- Need for personal development
- Important part of theory is that main motivating factors aren't in environment, but intrinsic value/satisfaction gained from job itself
- To motivate individual, job must have these requirements
	- Challenging
	- Have scope for enrichment
	- Be of interest
- Main hypothesis of theory was that certain factors lead to positive attitudes towards work, and others lead to negative attitudes
