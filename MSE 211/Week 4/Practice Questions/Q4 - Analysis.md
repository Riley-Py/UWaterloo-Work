Maslow, Herzberg, and Locke & Latham describe motivation at different levels.

Identify:
- One specific way Maslow or Herzberg helps explain behaviour
- One specific way they are incomplete without a mechanism (goal-setting)

## Response
Maslow explains behaviour through the fact that people prioritize meeting certain needs before other needs.  An example of this would be a person trying to get a job.  Before this person can do this, though, they first need to deal with the basic necessitities of life: they need to be well-fed and have a house.  Then, they need to have a solid base where they have a support system in-place and stability before focusing their energy into trying to get a job.  In this example, Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs can be applied, as before the person is motivated to get a job, they first have other needs that must be fulfilled before getting to that point of being motivated to get a job, like human needs.  Without having a solid foundation, the person wanting a job will not be able to fully focus on that goal with full attention.
