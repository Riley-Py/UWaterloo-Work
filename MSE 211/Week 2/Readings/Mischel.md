## Consistency/Specificity
- Behaviour can be predicted when you take into consideration the situation where the behaviour was performed
- Examined which aspects of behaviour were consistent and which weren't in all situations
	- Intellect is consistent
	- Personality isn't consistent (i.e. attitudes)
- If environment hasn't changed, then past behaviour can be a predictor of current behaviour
	- If environment has changed, then the behaviour isn't predictable
	- People can also change their behaviour overtime as well
