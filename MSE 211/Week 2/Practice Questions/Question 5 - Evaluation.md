## Question
Assess the claim: 

"Personality is a poor primary explanation for behaviour in real-world settings"

## Response
This claim is strongly supported by the readings as personality can change based on the environment.  Mischel found in his research into behaviour that the personality, and therefore behaviour, can change if the environment has changed.  An example of this would be in a social situation.  If I am with friends, I am far more likely to be more outgoing and extroverted with them.  However, if the environment changed and suddenly I was in an unknown setting with no familiar faces, then I would become much more reserved and introverted, even though my personality is extroverted and outgoing.
