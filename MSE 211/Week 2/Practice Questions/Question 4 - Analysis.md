## Question
Mischel and Kahneman both challenge simple explanations of behaviour, but from different angles.  Identify:

- One specific way Mischel's CAPS model and Kahneman's dual-process model reinforce each other
- One specific way they are in tension/incomplete relative to each other

## Response
A way that Mischel's Cognitive-Affective Processing System (CAPS) model and Kahenman's Systems model reinforce each other is the fact that each of them analyze how someone makes decisions given the situation.  Mischel's CAPS model describes how behaviour can be consistent/inconsistent based on the situation, while Kahneman's Systems model describes how different situations demand different Systems of thinking.  An example of this would be when someone is making a decision on what course they should take.  If they were allowed to take any course that they like, and say that this student was highly interested in computers, then the reasoning goes that they would automatically gravitate towards choosing a course that involves computers, even if that course turned out to not be what they thought it was.  The CAPS model predicted this, as this student's behaviour with computers was enthusiastic, so being presented with choosing a course, it was predictable that they would choose a computer course without hesitation.  The Systems model also describes this, as System 1 was involved with choosing that computer course immediately, with System 2 backing up that decision with the fact that this student was interested in the subject of computers.  The way that these two models are incomplete from one another is the fact that CAPS includes Cognitive-Affective Units (CAUs) that include belief systems and goals, while Kahneman's Systems model only describes the processing of decisions, not anything personal about the individual.  Back to the example of the student choosing courses, they may choose a computer course based on their behaviour, but the specific computer course could be based on their own personal beliefs.  They may think that cryptography is not it, so they decide to choose a algorithms course.  Mischel predicts this with his model, as it's part of their units; however, Kahneman's model wouldn't have predicted this without some further probing into the process.
