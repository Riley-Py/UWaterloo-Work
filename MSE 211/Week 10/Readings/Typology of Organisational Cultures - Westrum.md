- *Culture* - organisation's pattern of response to problems/opportunities it encounters
	- Shaped by unit's leaders
	- Set of processes that shapes organisational responses to challenges it faces
	- Challenges can be *explicit* (crisis) or *implicit* (a latent opportunity)
- Culture is like personality for a person; different for each unique item
- Culture involves pattern of thought, emotion, action

### Three Cultures Model
- Typology was created to characterize general ways of coping with information
	- Failures in info flow factor into major accidents
	- Info flow also factors into culture
- Leaders shape unit's culture; communicate what is important through actions and rewards/punishments
- Three patterns of preoccupation by leaders:
	- *Pathological* - power-oriented
	- *Bureaucratic* - rule-oriented
	- *Generative* - performance-oriented
	- Above creates climate
