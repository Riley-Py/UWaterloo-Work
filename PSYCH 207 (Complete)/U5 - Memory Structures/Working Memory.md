- Limited-capacity workspace that can be divided into storage and control processes
- It has three components:
	1. *Central executive* - directs the flow of information, which information will be operate on, when, and how.  Coordinates information from current environment with retrieval of info abut the past
	2. *Phonological loop* - carry out subvocal rehearsal for verbal material.  Important for language
		- Has a *phonological buffer* (holds info for a short time) and a *sub-vocal rehearsal loop* (compensates for rapid decay in info)
	3. *Visuospatial sketch pad* - Maintain visual material through visualization.  Mental images
	4. *Episodic buffer* - Needed when remembering specific events from the past and links info across domains
