- Long term memory consists of two systems that interact with each other
	- *Episodic memory* - holds memories of specific events in which you yourself somehow participated. For one's personal experiences and dated memories.  Organized as one event happening before or after.
	- *Semantic memory* - holds information that has entered your general knowledge base.  Stores general world knowledge and language.  Recorded as meanings and meaning relationships
- Episodic and sematic memory are *memory systems* that hold on to different kinds of information
