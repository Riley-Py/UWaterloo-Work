- Cash flow is changing by constant amount ($G$) each time
	- Examples: Costs that go up $500 each year, salary increases $2000 annually, production that ramps up 100 units monthly

## Future Worth

$$ F = G{{(1 + i)^n} - 1 - in \over i^2}$$
Where:
- $F$ is future worth
- $G$ is the amount going up per time period
- $i$ is interest rate
- $n$ is number of compounding periods

## Present Worth
$$ P = G {{(1 + i)^n - 1 - in} \over i^2(1 + i)^n}$$
Where:
- $P$ is present worth
- Rest of the variables are the same as future worth
