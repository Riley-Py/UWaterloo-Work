- Equal cash flows occurring at regular intervals
	- Example: Monthly mortgage payments, regular deposits into savings, annual insurance premiums, fixed salary payments
- Cash flows are equal (*uniform*)
- Intervals between flows are equal 
- Occur at end of each period
- Called an *annuity*

## Future Worth

$$ F = A{{(1+i)^n - 1 } \over i}$$
Where:
- $F$ is the future value
- $A$ is payment made at regular intervals
- $n$ is compounding periods
- $i$ is interest rate

## Present Worth

$$ P = A {{(1+i)^n - 1} \over i(1+i)^n}$$
Where:
- $P$ is the present value
- Rest of the variables same as future worth
