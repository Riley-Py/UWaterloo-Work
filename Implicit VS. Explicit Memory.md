- *Explicit memories* - consciously recollected (series of events or specific event)
- *Implicit memories* - not deliberate or conscious but shows evidence of prior learning/storage
## Priming 
- *Semantic priming* - exposure to one word leads to recognition of another word
- *Repetition priming* - facilitation of cognitive process of info after recent exposure to the same info (i.e. given a word for a short time, then having to fill in the blanks - called *word stem completion task*)
	- Nonwords show no or little repetition priming compared to real words
	- Greater for words that share roots of meaning than words that are visually similar
- *Korsakoff's syndrome* - Invented memories, amnesia of both types
