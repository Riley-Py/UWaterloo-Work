- *Basket of goods* - predetermined set of goods/services whose prices are tracked on a regular basis
	- Economy-wide price level is calculated as weighted average of price of products in basket of goods
- *Inflation* - increase in general price of goods and services
	- Future dollars are less valuable than present dollars (purchasing power decreases)
- *Deflation* - decrease in general price of goods and services

### Causes of Inflation
- *Increase in money supply*
	- Too much money compared to available goods/services, value of money decreases
	- Governments in war/election print excessive money
- *Cost-Push*
	- Cost of production goes up (oil price), companies increase price
- *Demand-Pull*
	- Increased demand leads to increased prices
