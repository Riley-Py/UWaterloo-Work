## Risk & Uncertainty
- Risk arises from uncertainty about future financial returns
	- *Risk* is quantifiable probability that actual return is different from expected return
	- *Uncertainty* is fundamental unpredictability of future outcomes

## Variability of Returns
- Simple measure is standard deviation of returns
	- *High standard deviation* - high risk, high return
	- *Low standard deviation* - low risk, low return
- Doesn't distinguish between company specific shocks from economy wide shocks
