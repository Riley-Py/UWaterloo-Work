
- Written records of financial activities of a company
- Three types
	- *Balance sheets* - Summary of company's financial position (assets/liabilities) at a specific time
	- *Income statement (profit/loss)* - Summary of company's financial performance (revenue/expenses) over a specific time period
	- *Cash flow statement* - Summary of cash inflows/outflows (operating, investing, financing) over a specific time period
