## How Firms Raise Capital?

### Debt Financing
- Companies borrow money with the knowledge that they have to repay
	- Bank loans 
	- Corporate bonds with coupon payments
	- Predetermined maturity dates
	- Interest payments regardless of company performance
### Equity Financing
- Companies sell ownership stakes to investors
	- Common stock representing ownership shares
	- No repayment schedule
	- Dividends paid at management discretion
	- Shareholders participate in company growth

## Debt Financing
- Borrowed capital that companies repay with interest
- Creates legal obligations/doesn't dilute ownership control
- Steps of debt financing
	1. *Company issues bond* - debt securities with specific terms
	2. *Investors provide capital* - bondholders lend money in exchange for securities
	3. *Periodic Interest Payments* - regular coupon payments throughout bond life
	4. *Principal Repayment* - full face value returned at maturity
### Bond Categories by Issuer Type
- *Sovereign bonds* - government-issued debt securities backed by national credit (i.e. U.S. Treasury Notes)
- *Supranational bonds* - issued by international organizations with multiple government backing (i.e. World Bank Bonds)
