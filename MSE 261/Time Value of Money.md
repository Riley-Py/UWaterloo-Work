## What Makes Money "Money"?
### Medium of Exchange
- Money must be widely accepted in exchange for goods/services
- People must be willing to receive it as payment with confidence that they can use it elsewhere

### Store of Value
- Money must retain purchasing power over time
	- Should be able to save it today and use it for purchases in the future without a significant loss of value
