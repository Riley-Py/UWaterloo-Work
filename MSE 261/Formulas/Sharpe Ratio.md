- Formula: $$S_a = {E \left [R_a - R_f\right] \over \sigma_a}$$
- Where:
	- $R_f$ is risk-free interest rate
	- Rest of the variables are the same from standard deviation of returns
- Simplified formula (if risk-free interest rate is 0): $$ \mu \over \sigma$$
- Where:
	- $\mu = E[R_a]$, the expected returns
