
 **Key Principles of Quantum Mechanics**
 **Particles and waves**  
   - Light behaves as particles called **photons**.  
   - Electrons and other particles exhibit **wave-particle duality**—they can behave like waves or particles depending on the situation.
**Discrete energy levels**  
   - Electrons in atoms can only occupy **specific energy levels**.  
   - This explains atomic spectra and chemical properties.
 **Atomic structure and fundamental particles**  
   - Atoms are mostly **empty space**.  
   - Protons and neutrons are **not fundamental**; they are made of **quarks**.  
   - **Antimatter** exists: particles with opposite properties that annihilate matter to produce energy.
 **Fundamental forces**  
   - Four forces govern all interactions:  
     - **Gravity** – universal, weak but long-range  
     - **Electromagnetism** – acts on charged particles, long-range  
     - **Strong nuclear force** – holds nuclei together, short-range  
     - **Weak nuclear force** – responsible for radioactive decay, short-range  
 **Quantum effects on technology and astronomy**  
   - Quantum laws underpin **modern technology**: computers, smartphones, internet, TV, radio.  
   - Quantum mechanics has **astronomical consequences**, e.g., properties of stars, black holes, and the early universe.
