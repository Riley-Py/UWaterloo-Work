**Spacetime**
- Space and time are **not absolute**; they form a 4-dimensional continuum.
- Motion mixes space and time, similar to rotating coordinates in space.
- Observers moving at different velocities see **different combinations** of space and time.
 **Curved Spacetime and Gravity**
- Straight-line motion can appear curved due to **spacetime curvature**.
- Gravity is **not a force** but the result of **curved spacetime**.
- Objects move along the straightest possible paths (geodesics) in curved spacetime.
**Matter-Curvature Relationship**
- **Mass and energy curve spacetime**.
- Greater mass → greater curvature → stronger gravitational effects.
- **Rubber sheet analogy**: heavy objects bend a sheet; spacetime bends similarly.
**Time in Gravitational Fields**
- Time runs **slower in stronger gravitational fields**.
- Similar to time dilation from high speeds in special relativity.
**Black Holes**
- Predicted by general relativity.
- Extremely dense regions with intense spacetime curvature.
- Represent one of the most non-trivial consequences of GR.
**Universe Structure**
- Universe may have **no boundaries or center** but can still have **finite volume**.
- Analogy: Earth's surface is finite but boundaryless.
**Gravitational Waves**
- Ripples in spacetime geometry.
- Propagate at the **speed of light**.
- Directly detected in **2016**.
- Generated by accelerating masses (e.g., merging black holes or neutron stars).
