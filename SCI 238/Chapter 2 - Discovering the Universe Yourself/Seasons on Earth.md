## What causes the seasons?
- Seasons depend on how Earth's axis affects the directness of sunlight
- The orientation relative to the sun changes as Earth orbits the Sun
- Winter occurs when the sunlight is less direct; summer occurs when the sunlight is more direct
### Sun's altitude also changes with seasons
- Sun's position at noon in summer: Higher altitude means more direct sunlight
- Sun's position at noon in winter: Lower altitude means less direct sunlight

## Why doesn't distance matter?
- Variation of Earth-Sun distance is small (~3%)
	- This variation is overwhelmed by the effects of axis tilt
