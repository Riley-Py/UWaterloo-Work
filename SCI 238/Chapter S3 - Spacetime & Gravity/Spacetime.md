- Object's number of dimensions is the number of independent directions in which movement is possible within the object
- Spacetime has 4 dimensions - X, Y, Z, and t (time)
- Space and time look different from different perspectives in spacetime
	- Space is different for different observers
	- Time is different for different observers
	- Therefore, spacetime is the same for everyone

## Worldlines
- Shows object's path through spacetime in a spacetime diagram
	- X-axis is the space
	- Y-axis is the time
	- Vertical - no motion
	- Diagonal - constant velocity
	- Curved - accelerating
- Diagram of this:
	- ![[Pasted image 20251109144045.png]]
- Wordlines for light go at 45 degree angles in diagrams with light-seconds (space) and seconds (time) on the axis
	- Diagram of this:
		- ![[Pasted image 20251109144205.png]]
- Worldlines look different in different reference frames
- Distance is constant regardless of reference frame
	- Formula: $$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - (ct)^2$$
## Rules of Geometry in Flat Space
- Straight line is shortest distance between two points
- Parallel lines stay the same distance apart
- Angles of triangle add up to $180 \degree$ 
- Circumference of circle is $2\pi r$
