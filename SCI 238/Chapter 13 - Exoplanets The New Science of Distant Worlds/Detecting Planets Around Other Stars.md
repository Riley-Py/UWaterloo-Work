## Challenges in Detecting Extrasolar Planets

### Brightness and Distance
- A sun-like star is about a billion times brighter than the light reflected from its planets
- Planets are close to their stars, relative to the distance from us to the star
- Ancient observers didn't think stars were like the Sun because the Sun is so much brighter
	- Christian Huygens used holes drilled in a brass plate to estimate angular sizes of stars
		- Showed that, if stars were like the Sun, they must be at great distances, consistent with the lack of an observed [[Paths of Eclipses and Planets|parallax]]
