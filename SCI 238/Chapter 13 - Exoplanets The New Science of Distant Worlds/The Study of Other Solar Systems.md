## The Formation of Other Solar Systems

### Revisiting the Nebular Theory
- Predicts that massive Jupiter-like planets should not form inside the frost line (at << 5 AU)
- Discovery of [[The Nature of Planets Around Other Stars|hot Jupiters]] has forced reexamination of the theory
- Planetary migration or gravitational encounters may explain hot Jupiters
