### How Can We Distinguish Science From Non-Science?
- Science is from the Latin phrase *scientia* (knowledge)
	- Not all knowledge comes from science
- Proven using the *scientific method*
	- Make observations
	- Ask question
	- Suggest hypothesis
	- Make prediction
	- Perform a test; experiment or additional observation

- Science rarely proceeds this way, however
	- Sometimes, we start by "just looking", then coming up with possible explanations
	- Sometimes, we follow our intuition
