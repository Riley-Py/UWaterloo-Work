## Patterns in the Solar System

### Features of Solar System Provide Clues to How it Formed

#### 1. Motion of Large Bodies
- All large bodies in the solar system orbit in the same direction and nearly same plane
- Most also rotate in that direction
#### 2. Two Major Planet Types
- *Terrestrial* - rocky, relatively small, close to the sun
	- Example: Earth
- *Jovian* - gaseous, larger, and farther from the Sun
	- Example: Jupiter
#### 3. Swarms of Smaller Bodies
- Many rocky asteroids and icy comets populate the solar system

#### 4. Notable Exceptions
- Earth's large moon
- Uranus' extreme tilt
- Position of Venus on Sun during transit depends on distances in solar system and position on Earth
