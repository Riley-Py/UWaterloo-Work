### How many Civilizations are out there?

#### The Drake Equation
- Number of civilizations with whom we could potentially communicate: $$ = N_{HP} \times f_{life} \times f_{civ} \times f_{now}$$
- Where $N_{HP}$ is total number of habitable planets in galaxy, $f_{life}$ is fraction of habitable planets with life, $f_{civ}$ is fraction of life-bearing planets with civilization at some time, and $f_{now}$ is fraction of civilizations now
	- $N_{HP}$ is probably billions
	- $f_{life}$ is hard to say (0 or 1)
	- $f_{civ}$ is unknown (took 4 billion years on Earth)
	- $f_{now}$ is unknown (depends on whether civilizations can survive long-term)
