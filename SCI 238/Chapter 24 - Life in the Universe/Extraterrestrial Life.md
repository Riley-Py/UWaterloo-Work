### Could there be life on Mars?
- No evidence that there was life on Mars
	- Could have been microbe life in the past
- Had liquid water in distant past
- Has subsurface ice/briny water
- *Curiosity rover* landed in 2012
	- Carries many instruments designed to explore habitability of planet
### Could there be life on Europa or other jovian moons?
- *Ganymede*, *Callisto*, *Europa* (Jupiter moons) show some evidence for subsurface oceans
- Little energy for life, but there could be enough 
- *Titan* (Saturn moon) is too cold for liquid water (could be some deep underground)
	- Lakes of liquid ethane/methane on surface
	- Has dunes
