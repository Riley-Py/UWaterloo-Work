## Thought Experiment: Train
- Someone on a train will see a ball thrown as going up and down
	- Someone outside the train would see the ball travel a longer path in an up-down cycle
	- Faster the train, the longer the path would be

### Time Dilation
- We can perform same thought experiment with light beam instead of ball 
	- Light beam moving at c travels a longer path in a moving object
	- Time must be passing slowly there
