## Mnemonics
- *Method of loci* - imaging a series of places that have a sort of order to them, and then you would divide the material you wanted to remember
- *Interacting images* - Forming images to help remember certain objects and places
- *Pegword method* - picturing items with another set of "ordered cues" (like rhyming)
- Another technique involves *recoding* material to *mediate* (go-between) your memory and the material

## The Dual-Coding Hypothesis
- Two coding systems for representing information to be stored
	- Verbal (linguistic meaning)
	- Imagery (mental pictures of some sort that represent what the item looks like)
	- Information can be coded by both
- Studies found that participants formed visual images of noun pairs
