For dissociating solutes, like $NaCl$, which produce two moles of ions in solution for each mole of solute dissolved, the effective molailty would be equal to twice the molailty.  It's accounted for by the *Van't Hoff Factor*, $i$:

$$\Delta T_b = iK_bm$$
$$\Delta T_f = -iK_fm$$
The Van't Hoff Factor is calculated by:

$$i = {{moles \ of \ particles \ present \ in \ solution }\over {mole \ of \ compound \ dissolved}}$$
