**Definition**:  It represents the number of electrons that an atom gains, loses, or otherwise appears to use in joining with other atoms in compounds.

## Rules for Assigning Oxidation States

- Oxidation state of any element that is not bounded to any other element is 0, such as $Cu, H_2, Cl_2$
- For a single ion, the oxidation state is equal to the charge of the ion.  $Cl^-$ has an oxidation state of -1, for instance
- The sum of the oxidation states of all atoms in a neutral, no charge compound is equal 0, while the sum of the oxidation states of all atoms in an ion is the charge of the ion
