## Demand Analysis
- If supply curve shifts, effect on price depends on price sensitivity of demand
- Impact of tax on competitive equilibrium price, tax revenue generated, and deadweight loss depend on price sensitivity of demand/supply
- For firm with market power, its optimal price depends on price sensitivity of demand for good
