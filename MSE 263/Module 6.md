## Costs
- *Accounting cost* - actual expenses plus depreciation charges for capital equipment
- *Economic cost* - Cost to a firm using economic resources in production, which includes *opportunity cost* (value of best alternative use of resource)
- *Sunk cost* - spending that can't be recovered

### Short-Run Cost
- *Fixed cost (F)* - production expense that doesn't vary with output
- In short run, there are large costs that are fixed
- Fixed and sunk costs are different
	- Examples of sunk fixed costs, related to newspapers: page design/layout, wages paid, costs of gathering/editing
	- Examples of non-sunk fixed costs: printing presses (can be sold), office space rent, long-term equipment
- *Variable cost $VC(Q)$* - cost that changes with quantity of output produced
- *Total cost (cost) ($C(Q)$)* - sum of firm's variable cost and fixed cost
	- $C = F + VC$
- *Marginal cost ($MC(Q)$)* - amount a firm's cost changes
