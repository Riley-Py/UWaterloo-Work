## Oligopoly
### Price Competition
- Demand curve depends on prices set by other firms; consider firms that produce imperfect substitutes
- Conditions
	1. Each firm has demand function that shows how demand for product depends on their price and other firms' prices
	2. Reaction curve of firm's is its optimal price as a function of other firms' prices
	3. Firms' decisions are interrelated
		- *Nash equilibrium* - prices charged by all firms that each firm's price maximizes its profit given prices of other firms; solve as system of equations
