## Pricing With Market Power
- *Perfect competition* - firm is price taker; believes it can sell any amount at market price
- *Imperfect competition* - firm has market power; sees trade-off between price/volume
- Sources of market power include
	1. *Differentiated products* - branded product is differentiated from other products
	2. *Homogenous goods* - firm is big player; increased output pushes down market price

### Marginal Revenue
- Extra revenue from producing/selling one extra unit has two aspects
	- Revenue increases by P if you sell 1 more unit of good
	- Producing/selling extra unit results in small drop in price ${\Delta P} \over {\Delta Q}$; reduces revenue from units sold
