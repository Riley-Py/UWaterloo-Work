## Perfectly Compeitive Market
- *Perfectly competitive market* - these conditions must hold:
	1. Many (small) buyers/sellers
	2. Homogenous (identical) product
	3. Perfect information on both sides of market
	4. No transaction costs
	5. In long run, free entry/exit
- Firms are price takers in perfect competition
	- *Short-term* - firms may earn profits/suffer losses
	- *Long-term* - entry/exit forces profits to 0
- Firms take market price as given due to following
	- *Firms sell identical products* - if goods are perfect substitutes, you can't raise prices; consumers stop buying
	- *Buyers/sellers know prices charged by firms* - can't raise price if consumers know what other firms are charging
	- *Firms enter/exit market* - can't raise price because firms can enter market and cut price
