## Price Discrimination

### Explicit Market Segmentation
- *Market segment* - subgroup of customers
- *Demand-based difference* - demand curves of each segment must be different
	- Charging a different price to different market segments raise firm's profit
- Price discriminating firm earns higher profit due to
	- Charging higher price to customers who are willing to pay more than uniform price, capturing surplus
	- Selling to people who were not willing to pay as much as uniform price
### Observability and No-Resale
- Customers avoid price discrimination by
	- *Masquerading* - pretending to be other segment
	- *Arbitrage* - buying product through customers in other segment
