## Four main factors affect reaction rates:

1. **Concentration of reactants** - increased concentration $\rightarrow$ increase in reaction rate.  More molecules means more [[Collision Theory|collisions]] 
2. **Reaction temperature** - increased temperature increases average kinetic energy of reacting molecules, and they have kinetic energy equal or exceeding the activation energy
3. **Presence of catalysts** - Catalysts lowers the activation of the reaction, and increases the rate of reaction, as shown by the graph below:
![[Pasted image 20241204173955.png]]
4. **Physical nature of reactants** - reactants in gaseous form or liquid form have greater freedom, and as a result, have more collisions with one another to form products.  With solids, it only happens on the surface.
