**Definition of relative humidity**: It's the ratio of the partial pressure of water vapour in the mixture to the equilibrium vapour pressure of water at that temperature with the formula: 
$$\% RH = {P_{H_2O} \over {P_{H_2O}}^{vap}(T)} \times 100 \%$$
