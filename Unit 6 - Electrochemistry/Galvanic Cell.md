**Definition**: Also known as a *voltaic cell*, derives energy from spontaneous redox reactions.  The batteries that are used for consumers are these cells.  

![[Pasted image 20241207151434.png]]

It contains two half cells.  The **anode** is the negative electrode and the oxidation half-reaction happens here.  The cathode is the positive electrode and the reduction reaction occurs here.  They are also connected by a salt bridge which contains an electrolyte to maintain electroneutrality.
