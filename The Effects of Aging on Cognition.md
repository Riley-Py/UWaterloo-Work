## Patterns of Preserved and Declining Functions
- Failing to recall something is called *prospective memory* (episodic/source also works)
- Performance on short-term memory tasks declines with age
	- Shows better performance with simple maintenance of information
- Seniors rely on *environmental support* (aids, hints, category headings)
- Memory for names/labels of objects, vocabulary, and word knowledge remain intact and increase over a lifespan
- People aging have strategies to compensate for the decline in ability:
	- Example: A pianist at 80 years old played fewer pieces (selection), practiced those pieces more often (optimization), and playing more slowly before fast segments to make the latter appear faster (compensation)
