**Definition**: It can be used to approximate the vapour pressure at a given temperature knowing the vapour pressure at another temperature and the enthalpy of vaporization.  Here is the formula: 
$$ln \left ({{P_2}^{vap} \over {P_1}^{vap}}\right) = - {{\Delta H_{vap}}\over R} \left({1 \over T_2} - {1 \over T_1}\right)$$
