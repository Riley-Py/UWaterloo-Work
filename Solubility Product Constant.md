The solubility, $S$, of a solid in a solvent is the concentration of the dissolved solid in a saturated solution at a given temperature

The solubility product constant, $K_{sp}$, is defined as the product of the equilibrium concentrations of the ions, each raised to the power of its stoichiometric coefficient.

With regards to the reaction quotient $Q_sp$:
- If $Q_{sp} > K_{sp}$, then precipitation is formed
- If $Q_{sp} = K_{sp}$, the solution is saturated and at equilibrium 
- If $Q_{sp} < K_{sp}$, the solution is unsaturated, if more solution is added, it will dissolve
