**Definition:** *reaction half-life*, $t_{1 \over 2}$ is the time it takes for one-half of the reactant to be consumed.  In terms:
$$C_A t_{1 \over 2} = {1 \over 2}C_{A_0}$$
- For a reaction that is zero-order in A (n = 0): 
$$ t_{1 \over 2} = {{C_{A_0}}\over 2k}$$
- For a reaction that is first-order in A (n = 1): 
$$ t_{1 \over 2} = {ln 2 \over k} = {0.693 \over k}$$
- For a reaction that is second-order in A (n = 2): 
$$t_{1 \over 2} = {1 \over {kC_{A_0}}}$$
