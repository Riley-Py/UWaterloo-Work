## Binomial
- Two outcomes only
- Bernoulli trials

## Multinomial
- More than two outcomes per trial

## Geometric
- Waiting until first success
- Memoryless property (related to exepotential/gamma)

## Negative Binomial
- Waiting until r-th success
- Related to gamma distribution

## Poisson
- Counting events in fixed interval
- Events occur randomly at constant rate

## Hypergeometric
- Sampling without replacement

## Multivariate Hypergeometric
- Sampling multiple categories without replacement
