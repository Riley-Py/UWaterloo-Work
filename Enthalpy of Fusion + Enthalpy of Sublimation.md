The temperature at which this phase transition (melting or fusion) takes place is the **melting point**

**Definition of Enthalpy of Fusion (soldification)**: Amount of heat that must be absorbed (released) to keep the solid at constant temperature when a given quantity melts (freezes) with this equation:
$$\Delta H_{fus} = H_{liquid} - H_{solid}$$
**Definition of Enthalpy of sublimation (deposition)**: Equal to the heat that must be abosrbed (released) to keep the solid at constant temperature when a given quantity undergoes sublimation (deposition).  The pressure exerted by vapours at equilibrium is called **sublimation pressure.** 

**NOTE**: Can use [[Clausius-Clapeyron Equation|equation]] with using $\Delta H_{sub}$
