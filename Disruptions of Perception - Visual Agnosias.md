- *Visual agnosias* - impairments on the ability to interpret (see) visual info
	- Patients suffering through this don't have a language problem or a memory problem; rather, it's understanding what the visual pattern/object is presented to them
		- They can't recognize objects by sight, but they could recognize them by sound, touch, or smell

- There are different categories for visual agnosias:
	- *Apperceptive agnosia* - patients can process contours/outlines/drawings, but can't match one object to another or categorize them
