The difference between this and the equilibrium constant is that the reaction quotient talks about the activities of the reaction at a specific point, not at equilibrium.

The equation for the reaction quotient in terms of concentration is:
$$Q_c = {{[C]^c[D]^d} \over {[A]^a[B]^b}}$$
In terms of pressure:
$$Q_p = {{{P_C}^c{P_D}^d}\over {{P_A}^a{P_B}^b}}$$
Some notes about reaction quotient:
- If reaction quotient is less than equilibrium constant - the reaction is to the right
- If reaction quotient is greater than equilibrium constant - the reaction is to the left
